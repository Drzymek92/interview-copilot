# AI Engineer (LLM & RAG) — Northwind Commerce  (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

> This is an invented job description for a fictional company, shipped as an example only.

## About the role
Northwind Commerce is building an internal assistant that answers operations and support
questions over the company's own documentation. We are hiring an AI Engineer to own the
retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline end to end — from ingestion and retrieval quality
through to serving and evaluation.

## What you'll do
- Own the RAG pipeline: chunking, embeddings, retrieval, and answer synthesis.
- Make answer quality **measurable** — build and maintain an offline evaluation harness and gate
  changes on it rather than on demos.
- Keep latency inside budget for an interactive assistant (p95 matters, not just the median).
- Work with an OpenAI-compatible LLM gateway; keep costs and prompt sizes honest.
- Collaborate with support and operations to turn their questions into a labelled eval set.

## What we're looking for
- Strong Python; comfortable owning a service end to end.
- Practical experience with LLM applications: retrieval, prompting, and (importantly) evaluation.
- A habit of measuring before claiming — you reach for a number, not a vibe.
- Clear written and spoken communication; able to disagree on evidence.

## Nice to have
- Experience with vector stores (pgvector, Qdrant, or similar).
- Exposure to experiment tracking (e.g. MLflow) and lightweight MLOps.
- Some fine-tuning experience (LoRA/adapters) — not required.

## Logistics
- Hybrid, with the interview conducted in Polish and English.
- Mid-level; the panel is one technical round plus a values/ways-of-working conversation.
