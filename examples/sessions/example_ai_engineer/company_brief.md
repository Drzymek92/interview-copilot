# Company brief — Northwind Commerce  (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

> Invented company, for the example bundle only. Nothing here refers to a real organisation.

**What they do.** Northwind Commerce runs a mid-size online marketplace. Support and operations
teams field a high volume of repetitive questions that are all answerable from existing internal
documentation — runbooks, policy pages, and product notes.

**Why the role exists.** They tried a naive "chat over our docs" prototype that demoed well but
gave confident wrong answers in front of real users, so it never shipped. They now want someone who
treats answer quality as something to **measure and defend**, not just demo.

**What they value (from the JD and public posts).**
- Evidence over opinion; a bias toward measuring.
- Ownership — one person accountable for the pipeline end to end.
- Honesty about limitations, especially where a wrong answer reaches a customer.

**Talking points that will land.**
- The evaluation-harness story (`star_rag_eval`) is the single most relevant thing to lead with.
- The reporting-automation story (`star_report_automation`) shows the same instinct applied to a
  simpler problem: remove the manual step, make it reproducible, leave an audit trail.
- The tail-latency story (`star_latency`) shows you think about what users feel (p95), not just
  averages.
